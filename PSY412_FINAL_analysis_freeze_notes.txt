- Do not treat bracket discrimination accuracy as an antisaccade success or attentional-capture measure.

EYE TRACKING
- Tobii Pro Nano sampling is approximately 60 Hz (~16.7 ms/sample).
- Tracking-valid and on-screen-valid are distinct; gaze points outside normalized screen bounds are not treated as on-screen.
- P003 has poor on-screen trial-window coverage; flag rather than automatically repair.
- Candidate I-VT events are exploratory, not validated saccades. Directional rates are conditional on detected candidates and QC-passing trials, not trial-level capture-error prevalence.
- Manual inspection was limited; no validated primary oculomotor outcome is established. Report eye-tracking findings as exploratory/descriptive or state the measure could not be validated.
